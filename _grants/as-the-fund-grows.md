As the fund’s work develops, I’ll share updates about organizations receiving grants. An entry may describe an organization and its work, what the fund has recommended or supported, and how I assessed that work: for example, its track record, local presence, and ability to reach people. Where I have a result or follow-up I can substantiate, I’ll include it.
