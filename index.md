# The Panou Fund

The Panou Fund advances liberty and relieves human suffering.

I started The Panou Fund on a simple conviction: individual action matters. Government programs and large institutions have their place, but the problems we face are ultimately answered by individuals who choose, freely and at their own expense, to do something about them.

The fund's work today runs through three initiatives: Liberty, Philoptochos, and Ukraine. Each reflects a conviction that freedom and charity are not abstract ideas, but practical responsibilities that become real through personal action.

The fund is designed to make giving simple, strategic, and focused. I handle the administrative work so that contributions can move quickly to organizations with proven impact, and I commit my time to finding the opportunities where support will make a meaningful difference.
