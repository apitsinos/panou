The Panou Fund is advised by Alex Pitsinos. After considering different philanthropic structures and meeting with DonorsTrust's leadership, he chose DonorsTrust as the fund's home.

Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Alexandria, Virginia, DonorsTrust is a public charity that sponsors charitable giving accounts and commits to honoring each fund's charitable intent during the lifetime of the advisor and beyond. Its structure also makes it easy for others to give alongside the fund. Read more about [how DonorsTrust works](https://www.donorstrust.org/how-it-works/).

## How contributions work

Contributions to The Panou Fund are made to DonorsTrust and are deductible to the extent allowed by law. A donor may contribute cash, securities, or other assets to the fund and generally receives an immediate tax deduction for the contribution.

DonorsTrust holds and controls the assets. It performs due diligence to confirm that each grantee is a qualified public charity, and handles compliance, record keeping, and reporting. Alex recommends grants consistent with the fund's intent, and DonorsTrust reviews and approves each recommendation.

The fund was started in the belief that individual action matters and that people of goodwill can accomplish far more together than any of us could alone. Whether you're a longtime friend, family, or someone Alex hasn't yet had the pleasure of meeting, he'd welcome the chance to partner with you.
