The Panou Fund is advised by me, Alex Pitsinos. I chose DonorsTrust as the fund’s home after careful consideration of different philanthropic structures and the trust I felt after meeting with DonorsTrust’s leadership.

Founded in 1999 and headquartered in Alexandria, Virginia, DonorsTrust is a public charity that sponsors charitable giving accounts and commits to honoring each fund’s charitable intent during the lifetime of the advisor and beyond.

DonorsTrust’s structure also makes it easy for others to give alongside me.

I started this fund because I believe individual action matters and because people of goodwill can accomplish far more together than any of us could alone. Whether you’re a longtime friend, family member, or someone I haven’t yet had the pleasure of meeting, I’d welcome the chance to partner with you.

Contributions to The Panou Fund are made to DonorsTrust and are deductible to the extent allowed by law. A donor contributes cash, securities, or other assets to the fund and generally receives an immediate tax deduction for the contribution.

DonorsTrust holds and controls the assets. They perform due diligence to confirm that each grantee is a qualified public charity, and handle compliance, record keeping, and reporting.

I recommend grants consistent with the fund’s intent, and DonorsTrust reviews and approves each recommendation.
