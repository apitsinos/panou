## For a free people

A sovereign, democratic Ukraine is a vital interest for anyone who cares about the international order, the rule of law, and peace in Europe. This initiative supports organizations working to sustain Ukraine and its people through the war. My Ukrainian friends and my friends who spend time in Ukraine tell me about the dire need for supplies and emergency relief.

How difficult daily life is in wartime. Medical supplies and essential medicine. Reliable transportation and ambulances. Prosthetics. Stoves and heaters. Communication devices. Non-lethal defensive gear.

Voluntary generosity must rise to meet urgent need, and the people of Ukraine face it daily. I support organizations with established operations in Ukraine and a demonstrated capacity to reach those most affected by the war. I work with folks on the ground to ensure impact.
